# Maintainer simulation reports

Maintainer simulation PDFs contain results in the established approved format;
reuse only approved explanatory comments; keep necessary result headings and
short captions in the established style; never add explanatory notes,
methods/provenance/review commentary or caveats unless explicitly requested for
the PDF. Extend results without changing the simulation system. Technical
discussion belongs in chat/PR/existing diagnostic artifacts, not automatically
in the document.

In the full report, new prior variants join the corresponding existing test
sections, settings and analysis-prior tables, and pooled result plots.
Incremental reports select only affected sections and show results only for
the new priors. Render both reports natively from complete cached inputs.
Preserve the approved report's table and section structure in both scopes.
Keep additional diagnostics in machine-readable artifacts only.

Do not restore the report prose removed on 2026-11-04: the Normal mean/SD
sentence, Normal sequential schedule and rare-event discussion/example,
run-specific t discrepancy summary, search-panel interpretation, detailed
fixed/sequential search-condition paragraphs, or baseline worker-count
statement. Preserve other approved text unless explicitly changed.
